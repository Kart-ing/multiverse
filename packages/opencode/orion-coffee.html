Our Story

Crafted for the Night Owl

Orion Coffee Shop was born from a simple idea: the best conversations happen after dark. Inspired by the constellation that guides travelers home, we created a space where coffee, community, and cosmos converge.

Every bean we source is ethically traded and small-batch roasted. Our baristas are artisans — trained to pull the perfect shot, steam velvet microfoam, and make you feel at home the moment you walk through the door.
