Rooted in craft,
grown by community.

We started in a 300-square-foot garage with a refurbished Probat roaster and a belief that great coffee shouldn't be complicated. Ten years later, we still roast every batch by hand — by sight, smell, and sound — the way it was meant to be done.

Our beans come from farms we visit every harvest. We pay above Fair Trade, build multi-year relationships with growers, and keep our supply chain short. When you drink Aroma, you're tasting a direct connection to the soil, the farmer, and the roaster.
